Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions, or send us your own query.

What documents are required to purchase a car? −

You generally need CNIC, proof of income, bank statement, and a recent utility bill. Requirements may vary depending on payment method and dealer policy.

Can I book a test drive before purchasing a car? ＋

Yes, many dealerships allow you to schedule a test drive. Use the contact or inquiry form on our platform, and we’ll help connect you to the relevant dealer.

Are the cars brand-new or used? ＋

Our listings may include both brand-new and used vehicles. Each car card clearly mentions model year, mileage (for used cars), and condition.

Do you provide after-sales services or warranty? ＋

CarsArena itself is a platform. Warranty and after-sales services are provided by authorized dealers or manufacturers, and details are mentioned with the respective car.

Can I compare multiple cars side by side? ＋

Yes. Our comparison option lets you select multiple cars and view their specifications, prices, and key features in one place.
